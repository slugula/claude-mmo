## Overview

Starting items are given to every new player when they first spawn. They provide the minimum tools required to engage with the earliest gathering and combat content.

## Current Starting Items

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Iron Pickaxe | Required to mine copper, tin, and iron ore |
| Bronze axe | Required to chop regular trees |

## Rules

- Starting items are placed in the player's inventory on first spawn
- If the inventory is full (impossible at spawn), excess items are dropped at the spawn tile
- Starting items are not uniquely marked — they can be dropped, traded, or lost like any other item

## Future Considerations

- Starting items may expand as new skills are added (e.g., a tinderbox for Firemaking)
- A tutorial area may replace direct item grants once onboarding content exists
