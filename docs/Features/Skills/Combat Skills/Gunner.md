The Gunner is a ranged-based class who attacks with large, two-handed energy weapons. They also wear heavy metallic armor making them substantially more resistant to damage than other combat styles.

A Gunner's primary weapon is the Chaingun which focuses on dealing low amounts of damage with a high rate of fire. Chainguns are powered by a unique ammo type called Kinetic Charges. Kinetic charges are stackable, meaning a player can hold multiple Kinetic Charges on a single slot, and the amount available in that stack is denoted by a small number in the top-right of the item icon.

Chainguns attack at a relatively high speed of every 2 ticks.

## Gaining XP
Players gain Gunner XP by dealing damage with a Gunner weapon. The total XP received is equal to the damage dealt x 4.
