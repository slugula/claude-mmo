Reduces incoming hit chance and determines what equipment (of any style) can be equipped. Defense does not reduce the actual damage taken, just how likely it is to take damage in the first place.

## Gaining XP
Players can train their Defense stat by choosing to lower their maximum damage output via switching their attack style to a defensive one. While in this style, players will gain XP equal to the damage dealt x 4.

Where and how players switch their attack style will come at a later time.
