
## Overview
Woodcutting is a gathering skill that primarily involves chopping trees to obtain logs. The higher a player's woodcutting level, the faster they are at obtaining resources.
## Interacting with the Skill
Trees come in a variety of types and are found in the overworld. In order to interact with a tree, the player must have a woodcutting axe equipped in their main hand or present in their inventory. In order for the player to use the woodcutting axe, they need to have the required woodcutting level to wield or use it.

Players can begin chopping a tree by clicking it, after which they will roll against the skilling success function every 12 game ticks to determine whether they successfully receive a resource or not.

Trees despawn on a timer which starts on the first tick of interaction with the tree. If player stop cutting the tree before fully chopped, the tree will regenerate with every passing second until the timer is zero again. Once the despawn timer reaches zero, the tree will deplete on the next successful resource role, disappearing and leaving only a stump behind. It will then take the Respawn Time for the tree to return.
